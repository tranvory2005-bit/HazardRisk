How to read this report. This is a diligence screening tool, not a substitute for a flood elevation certificate, wind mitigation inspection, geotechnical soil boring/sinkhole testing, or licensed insurance quote. Flood zone data is from FEMA NFHL; wind estimates are derived from coastal climatology; wildfire, earthquake, and tornado ratings are from FEMA's National Risk Index; sinkhole risk is evaluated at the specific coordinate level using USGS National Karst bedrock layers, Florida Geological Survey Subsidence Incident records, Pennsylvania DCNR, and Kentucky Geological Survey LiDAR inventories.
